Barnette's conjecture asserts that every finite, simple, cubic, bipartite,
planar, 3-connected graph has a Hamiltonian cycle. It remains one of the
central open problems about Hamiltonicity in planar graphs.

This submission presents the conjecture as an open theorem statement over
graphs with vertex set `Fin n`. Four separate definition concepts introduce
3-connected graphs, cubic graphs, bipartite graphs, and Hamiltonian cycles.
Planarity is deliberately not duplicated: the conjecture imports the
straight-line-drawing predicate from the Planar Graph Classes submission
(`lax-68`). No proof is supplied.

Recent progress proves the conjecture when every face has size at most $8$.
The next natural restricted case is maximum face size $10$.
