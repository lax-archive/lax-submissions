**This submission contains no mathematics of its own.** It is a synthetic
benchmark for the archive website's proof-network drawing: a submission whose
only purpose is to exhibit, in one place and under stable names, every shape
that figure has to lay out.

Every statement here is one rung of a single trivial ladder. `Stage k n` means
that $n$ is divisible by $2^k$, and each of the 53 statements asserts
`Descent a b`, that divisibility by $2^a$ implies divisibility by $2^b$ for
some concrete $a \ge b$. All of them are true, and every proof genuinely
applies the statements it declares as assumptions — the mathematics is just
thin enough that the *dependency graph* can be wired into any shape at all.

The 30 concepts are named after the shapes they exercise: sibling proofs whose
conclusion sits at the left, the middle and the right of a dock row; a proof
mixing a sibling assumption with a foreign one; chains of sibling proofs; a
statement with two proofs and another with three; a twelve-dock concept with an
eleven-wide assumption rail; a proof assuming one statement from each of eight
other concepts; a genuine two-concept cycle and a one-element self-cycle, both
of which stay unproven; an open statement with an open chain below it; nine
single-statement concepts in a linear chain; an isolated concept with no edges
at all; deliberately overlong and non-ASCII names; and two statements
discharged from `lax-242665`, so that external nodes appear as well.

Nothing here should be cited, endorsed or built upon. It is a ruler.
