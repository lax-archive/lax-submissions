This reference guide collects existing Lax definitions of graph classes,
containment relations, width parameters, and sparsity measures. Its chapters
group the source concepts by subject. In the paper view, each marked passage
opens the original concept and its Lean formalization beside the PDF.

The submission declares no concepts, statements, or proofs of its own. The
referenced definitions retain their original submission identities; similar
names in different submissions are not identified with one another here.
